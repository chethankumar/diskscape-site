Questions, answered

Is DiskScape free?

Yes — DiskScape 1.0 is free while in beta. The full app, including the duplicate finder and the uninstaller, is included in the download.

Does DiskScape delete my files?

No. DiskScape never deletes in place. Staged items are reviewed in a cleanup queue, then moved to the macOS Trash, where everything stays recoverable.

How does it find duplicates?

Files are grouped by exact size, hashed with a 64 KiB partial SHA256, then fully hashed — matches are byte-for-byte identical. You choose the keeper; stage the rest.

Is my Mac supported?

Any Apple Silicon Mac on macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later. The download is ~4 MB, signed with a Developer ID certificate and notarized by Apple.
